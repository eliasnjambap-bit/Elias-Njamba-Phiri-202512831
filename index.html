About Me

My name is Elias Njamba Phiri, and I am a second-year Computer Science student at Mulungushi University. I completed my secondary education at Northmead Secondary School, and I currently live in Matero North. I would describe myself as a hardworking, determined, and curious person who is always willing to learn and improve. I enjoy spending time with friends, learning about technology, and exploring new ideas that can help me grow as a person.

I chose to study Computer Science because I have always been interested in computers and technology and how they can be used to solve problems and make people's lives easier. Since joining university, I have learned more about programming, computer systems, problem-solving, and different areas of technology. Although some topics can be challenging, I enjoy the learning process because it pushes me to think differently and improve my skills.

As a student, I am also learning how to manage my time, take responsibility for my decisions, and become more independent. I believe that university is not only about getting good grades but also about developing discipline, confidence, communication skills, and practical knowledge. My goal is to continue working hard, gain more experience in technology, and eventually build a successful career in the IT and Computer Science field. I also hope to use the knowledge and skills I gain to create useful solutions that can help people in my community and beyond.
